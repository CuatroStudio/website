Tildone privacy policy

Tildone stores notes and tasks on your devices. When you use iCloud sync, a copy is stored in the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account so your devices can receive the same changes. This includes note and task text, formatting, order, completion, note appearance, and identifiers and versions needed for synchronization. Tildone also stores an installation identifier and basic device-platform information to support sync and the device summary. The developer cannot access your private iCloud notes and does not operate a database of your content.

Device settings and local sync state stay on your devices. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Tildone does not operate a separate payment or purchase-account service.

Pausing sync keeps cached notes and changes waiting to be sent on that device. It does not delete local or iCloud data. When sync resumes, changes can reach your other devices. Deleting a note, including automatic removal after completing a checklist, propagates when sync is available. Deletion markers and retained local migration copies may remain to protect synchronization and recovery; deletion is not a promise of immediate erasure of every retained copy. Removing the app does not itself delete its iCloud data. You can manage iCloud storage in Apple's settings.

Tildone has no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting service. The developer only views aggregate usage and diagnostic statistics supplied by Apple through App Store Connect, without exporting or retaining identifiable reports. Apple may process service and diagnostic information under its own privacy settings and policies.

Contact hello@cuatro.studio with privacy questions. If you email us, your email service transmits the information you choose to include. We do not maintain a support archive or retain support emails, attachments or diagnostic reports. Tildone does not automatically send your notes or diagnostic reports to the developer.

Tildone 隐私政策

Tildone 将笔记和任务存储在你的设备上。使用 iCloud 同步时，副本会存储在与你的 Apple 账户关联的私有 iCloud 数据库中，让你的设备接收相同的更改。这些数据包括笔记和任务文本、格式、顺序、完成状态、笔记外观，以及同步所需的标识符和版本信息。Tildone 还会存储安装标识符和基本的设备平台信息，用于同步和设备概览。开发者无法访问你的私有 iCloud 笔记，也不会运营存储你内容的数据库。

设备设置和本地同步状态保留在你的设备上。购买由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理；Tildone 不提供独立的付款或购买账户服务。

暂停同步会在该设备上保留缓存的笔记和等待发送的更改，不会删除本地或 iCloud 数据。恢复同步后，更改可以传到你的其他设备。删除笔记（包括完成清单后自动移除笔记）会在同步可用时传播到其他设备。为了保护同步和恢复，删除标记以及迁移时保留的本地副本可能继续存在；删除并不代表所有保留副本都会立即清除。卸载应用本身不会删除其 iCloud 数据。你可以在 Apple 的设置中管理 iCloud 储存空间。

Tildone 不使用第三方分析、广告、跟踪或崩溃报告服务。开发者仅查看 Apple 通过 App Store Connect 提供的汇总使用和诊断统计，不导出或保留可识别个人的报告。Apple 可能按照自身的隐私设置和政策处理服务及诊断信息。

如有隐私问题，请联系 hello@cuatro.studio。发送邮件时，你的邮件服务会传输你选择提供的信息。我们不维护支持档案，也不保留支持邮件、附件或诊断报告。Tildone 不会自动向开发者发送你的笔记或诊断报告。
